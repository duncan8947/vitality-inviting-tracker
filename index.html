Coach Guidance

Use this tracker to stay consistent, spot your conversion rates, and avoid last‑minute “oh sh*t” moments.
Daily (2 minutes):
  • Log your inviting actions as they happen (tap +1).
  • When you take a phone number from street inviting, log it under Phone numbers gained.
  • When a wellness evaluation is booked, log it under the source. The overall total is calculated automatically.
  • For street bookings, also log which follow‑up closed it: #1 / #2 / #3 / #4.
What counts as an “invite”?
  • Street: a real conversation where you offered a next step.
  • Social: a real DM/invite message sent (not a like).
  • Guinea pig: a direct invite to trial/support.
Follow‑up numbers: #1 is the first follow‑up after the initial invite, #2 the next, etc.

Mindset: This is not a “pressure tool”. It’s awareness. If you’re behind pace, you don’t panic — you adjust early.
